## Bolter connection and delivery metadata

These tables support only the admitted `plaintext_only` integration. They are
not field-encrypted. Provider and local identifiers can link people, devices,
conversations and activity; content fingerprints do not anonymize those links.
Operator storage and backup protection remains necessary. Message text and
external display labels stay in the canonical transcript, not these tables;
Desktop credentials and encrypted receive spools stay on the owning Desktop.
This inventory does not assert protected-mode support or whole-product
confidentiality. Forced RLS constrains the restricted agent role; the product
role may bypass RLS, so its paths also require explicit owner predicates and
mutation guards. Execution tokens are private authority material, not UI data.
